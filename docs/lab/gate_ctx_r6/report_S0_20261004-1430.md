C1e's 10 % tolerance and C1f's floor are lab conventions. They have no literature source, and the file says so.

## Addendum (2026-10-05): convergence-check fixes after review; retro C1 recomputed

An adversarial review (workflow `wf_3a789fa2-71a`) confirmed four defects in `vsl_lab/train/convergence.py` and the trainer:
