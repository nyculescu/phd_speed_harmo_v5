## Reading (2026-10-09)
- **All 5 cells: KILL** under the pre-registered rule. In 4 cells even the cheating oracles gain ≤ 0.2 % over no control; no control is itself the best non-learning controller.
- **Z9 (zipper merge, ramp 900, 3,600 veh/h) is the only hint.** Medians: nc 260 s, constant VSL 0.9 236 s, O2 192 s. By the binding convention (median of paired per-seed relative differences) H = 5.8 % [0.0, 27.0] → KILL. The ratio of the medians would give 18.6 %. The prereg sentence names both forms; the code and CLAUDE.md use the paired one, which decides. The gap arises because at a 50 % breakdown share the oracle helps only on the seeds that break down.
- S1a shares are non-monotone in demand (e.g. M3), so 10 seeds per level are noisy; capacity drops in S1a are 0.87–0.96 (inside the realism band 0.82–0.97).
- Per the prereg, no cell licenses the length sweep. Anything further on Z9 (more seeds, conditional-on-breakdown headroom) is a new pre-registered stage, the author's call.
