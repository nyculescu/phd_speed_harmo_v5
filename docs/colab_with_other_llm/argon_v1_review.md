## Full-text claims about Papageorgiou et al. (2008)

Argon says it read this paper in full (a PDF the author provided). The claims are useful, but **none is verified by us yet**:

1. Bay Bridge toll-plaza signals and tunnel-entrance control as field precedents (§2.5, §3.1, §5). This would replace the dropped Haboian.
2. All-lane mainstream signals 50 m upstream of a 3-to-1 lane drop; 4-s green, 2-s minimum red, ALINEA with T = 30 s; metering *all* lanes for equity and safety (§4).
3. Integral feedback gives stationary accuracy despite implementation errors such as **red-light violations** (§2.7.1).
4. Large run-to-run variance across 10 AIMSUN replications, with occupancy spikes (§4.2, Figs. 10–11).
5. Online critical-occupancy estimation cites Smaragdis et al. 2004 and Kosmatopoulos et al. 2006 (§2.7.1).
