Where may DRL have a purpose? A demand sweep

Exploratory, report-only (V406-V407). 16 demand levels drawn at random from 3,800-6,000 veh/h; steady traffic, X1 and X2; the 5 per-CAV PPO policies against no control, the fixed 0.8 s rule (p333), the best tuned rule (os1_15_10) and the single rule; 4 paired seeds per level. The policies were trained at 4,090-5,300; the rules were tuned at 5,300 only.

How to read. Every chart shows how much better DRL is, in %: above the zero line, DRL is better; below it, DRL is worse. For close-following (TTC), better means fewer seconds where a car would reach the one ahead within 3 s; for delay, it means less lost time. Thin dotted lines are single levels (4 seeds, noisy); thick lines are a running median over 3 neighbouring levels.
