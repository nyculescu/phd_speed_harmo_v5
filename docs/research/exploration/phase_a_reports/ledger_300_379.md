## (d) Reasons outside H1-H5

- The plant absorbs disturbances: brake, truck, burst, outage form no wave (V342); "no plant here has a capacity drop or a condition changing inside a run" (V304).
- At 4,600 flow is capped by demand (99.4 % served, V305, V307).
- Training stalls early (V364); entropy about 0.5 (V337). INFERENCE: an optimisation or reward-shape limit, not budget.
- Delay, the primary metric, "was in no bar" before V307. Reward terms named in these rows: safety (TTC), flow, vehicles in system, braking, a small charge per change (V309, V338, V367); no door-to-door delay term is named. INFERENCE: the learner was rewarded on proxies of the headline metric; the full reward definition is not in these rows.

- Artefacts a controller could exploit or be hurt by: sawtooth (V324), hidden braking (V367), V2V tau jump (V377).
- Exposure favoured DRL in confirmatory 2 and it still tied (V359 limits).
