- The splits are marginal (road, speed band and period separately), not joint, so I cannot say which speed band the 4a excess falls in, or which period the edge-3 excess falls in.
- Band exposures come from 5-km/h step histograms, with the 15-20 and 35-40 km/h bins split uniformly. Edge and period exposures come from the mainline space-time field (cell mean speed; ramps excluded; 92-98 % of vehicle-seconds), while the TTC counts include ramps.
- The no-control-calibrated regressions are model-based. v3w's jam share (0.087-0.205) lies partly below no control's range (0.145-0.375), so they extrapolate. The X2 band model has a negative hi-band coefficient. Use them only as corroboration of the Kitagawa result.
- The 'rest' road group lumps edges 1, 2, 3a, 4, 5, ramp 1 and junction lanes. The X2 offset is placed at merge 1 through the separate E30 counter (merge-1 lanes), not through the road split.
- The X1-X2 contrast pairs runs by seed number. I assume, without checking, that the same seed gives the same demand realisation in both cells; an unpaired comparison would widen the CIs.
- Per-seed correlations of paired differences are confounded by the shared no-control run and seed; even the shuffled-nc null is only an approximate null, because pairing also carries seed-level co-movement.
- About 2,870 CIs and tests were computed (results.json 1,504; deeper.json 1,164; third.json 180; merge1.json 20), most of them descriptive. None is corrected for multiplicity; 'CI excludes 0' is the only strength statement used. Nothing here supports a thesis claim.
